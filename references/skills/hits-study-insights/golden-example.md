# Golden example: team AI use

## Prompt

> How can I make sure my team uses AI?

## Expected answer qualities

- Opens with a short cross-study synthesis.
- Avoids promising that a manager can force durable adoption.
- Uses clear, declarative section headings.
- Combines quantitative evidence with practical implications.
- Places source links directly beneath the section they support.
- Does not show ranking diagnostics unless requested.

## Example

## Cross-study summary

You cannot force durable AI use. Across the selected studies, adoption rises when
leaders model AI use, employees have approved tools and practical support, and teams
can experiment and critically evaluate AI output. Make AI useful in real work rather
than simply mandatory.

**Sources:** [AI Meta-Cognition](https://hits.microsoft.com/study/6048090);
[Perceived AI Value at Work](https://hits.microsoft.com/study/6048902);
[Transformational Leadership in the Age of AI](https://hits.microsoft.com/study/6048446);
[HR & IT Collaboration](https://hits.microsoft.com/study/6047160);
[AI Friction as a Mastery Challenge](https://hits.microsoft.com/study/6047508)

### Model the behavior you expect

Use AI visibly, share useful prompts, and explain when you reject or correct its
output. Employees who felt supported in integrating AI were 1.8 times as likely to
use it daily.

**Source:** [Transformational Leadership in the Age of AI](https://hits.microsoft.com/study/6048446)

### Give the team approved tools and concrete use cases

Remove access barriers and connect AI to recurring tasks. Low access to
organization-sponsored tools was associated with up to a 17% drop in realized AI
value.

**Source:** [HR & IT Collaboration](https://hits.microsoft.com/study/6047160)

### Make learning part of the work

Pair role-specific instruction with coaching and immediate practice. Employees who
encountered difficulty but still realized value were 1.8 times as likely to feel
adequately trained and 1.9 times as likely to be high-frequency AI users.

**Source:** [AI Friction as a Mastery Challenge](https://hits.microsoft.com/study/6047508)

### Reward judgment, not raw usage

Ask employees to verify output and discuss limitations. Critical reflection about AI
was associated with individual AI value (`r = .748`) and team AI value (`r = .597`).

**Source:** [AI Meta-Cognition](https://hits.microsoft.com/study/6048090)

## Anti-patterns

- Ranking by titles.
- Selecting globally similar studies outside the curated group.
- Allowing raw occurrences of `manager` or `AI` to dominate ranking.
- Reporting exact corpus frequencies from incomplete or truncated bodies.
- Treating repeated wording as stronger causal evidence.
- Moving all citations to a detached list at the end.
